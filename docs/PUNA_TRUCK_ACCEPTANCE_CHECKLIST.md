# PUNA truck walkthrough — José + one worker, about 12 minutes

Use two labelled pretend orders and test food/empty bags. Do this when the team is ready; nothing below is signed off yet. Record the date, José, worker, tablet and any change needed. Never take a real payment for this walkthrough.

| Time | Try together | Agree before signing off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 min | Put the tablet where the worker will use it. Start a pretend shift and check the displayed count. | Who owns, charges and cleans it? Can both people see/reach it without interrupting food handling? |
| 2–4 min | Receive one paid pretend order while serving a walk-in. Label and physically separate its food; check items, set aside and pack. | Where do protected bags go? Who responds to the alert? Are the counter buttons workable during a rush? |
| 4–6 min | Make a mixed order: one ready item and one still baking. Mark only part of the batch ready, then delay or waste an item. | No early Ready message or silent substitution. Who may correct counts, waste or resolve a shortage? Keep frozen/legacy food separate. |
| 6–8 min | Scan a test pass, inspect it, then explicitly confirm Handed to customer. Try the same pass again and try manual lookup. | A scan alone changes nothing; one handover only. What does the worker ask when the customer's phone dies? |
| 8–10 min | Disconnect the tablet, reconnect and hand the shift to the second worker. | New sales stop when stock is unconfirmed. Recount/review before physical actions; do not repeat an uncertain action blindly. Are 90-second heartbeat and 15-minute stock review practical? |
| 10–12 min | Discuss an order waiting too long, a late customer and a payment/shortage exception. | Confirm actual hours, safe holding policy, pickup windows and named cancellation/refund/substitution authority. Never automatically resell paid food. |

For each row mark **Works / Change needed / Not tested**, with one short note. Any unsafe or confusing step stays open. José and the worker approve operations only after the changes are tried again. Technical tests alone do not sign off the truck workflow.
